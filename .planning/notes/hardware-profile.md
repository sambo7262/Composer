# User Hardware Profile

**NAS:** Synology DS423+
**CPU:** Intel Celeron J4125 (x86_64/amd64, 4-core, 2.0GHz burst 2.7GHz)
**RAM:** 32GB
**Architecture:** amd64 — pre-built Essentia wheels work, no ARM64 concerns
**Docker network:** synobridge (shared by Composer, Plex, Lidarr, Ollama)
**Music library:** 10k+ tracks managed by Lidarr, served by Plex
**Remote access:** Tailscale VPN — all NAS apps accessible outside LAN, no auth/HTTPS needed in the app
