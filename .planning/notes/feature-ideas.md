# Feature Ideas — Quick Interaction & Playlist Generation

All ideas fit the chat-first philosophy — shortcuts into the conversation, not separate features.

## Mood Presets / Quick Buttons
Row of quick-tap mood buttons on the chat page: "Chill", "Energetic", "Focus", "Late Night", "Workout". One tap → instant playlist. Refine via chat after. Conversation starters, not replacements.

**Phase:** 4 (part of chat UI)

## "What's Playing" Context
If user is listening in Plexamp, app knows what's currently playing and offers "More like this" as a one-tap action. Bridges passive listening → active curation.

**Phase:** v2+ (requires Plex session/now-playing API integration)

## Playlist Templates
Save a successful prompt as a reusable template: "Sunday morning coffee" becomes a recipe. Hit it weekly, get a fresh playlist each time (AI varies picks, new tracks may have been added).

**Phase:** 5 (playlist management) or v2

## Smart Shuffle into Existing Playlists
When new tracks land from Lidarr, proactively suggest: "3 new tracks match your 'Deep House' playlist. Add them?" Notification-style card on home page.

**Phase:** 5 (playlist management — ties into existing playlist analysis)

## Mood Timeline
Visual showing energy/tempo arc of a playlist. See if the flow builds up, stays flat, or winds down. "Make the energy build gradually" as a chat command.

**Phase:** 4 or 5 (depends on where the playlist visualization lives)

## "Surprise Me"
Zero-effort mode. No prompt, no mood — "give me something good based on what I haven't listened to recently." Discovers your own library.

**Phase:** 4 (chat command — simplest implementation, high delight)
