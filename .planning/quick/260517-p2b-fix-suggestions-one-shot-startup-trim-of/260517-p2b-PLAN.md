<objective>
Ship a one-shot, idempotent, lifespan-time startup trim that drops any
existing oversized `SuggestionsMirror` down to `SUGGESTIONS_TARGET_SIZE`
(30) on the next Composer boot. This unstalls the rate-feedback loop
on NAS installs that carried a 39-row mirror over from pre-`260517-nkt`
unconstrained cron runs.

Purpose: After `260517-nkt`, discovery WRITES are capped — but EXISTING
oversized mirrors are never cleaned. With `current=39 > target=30`,
`maybe_schedule_refill`'s `deficit = max(0, 30 - 39) = 0`, so no
`sql_refill` ever fires and the loop stays stalled. A one-shot
MigrationLog-gated trim — mirroring Phase 8.1 / 8.2 byte-for-byte —
fixes the carryover state once and stays out of the way thereafter.

Output: New `PHASE_08_3_MIGRATION_ID` constant, new
`run_phase_08_3_trim_suggestions_mirror_to_target()` coroutine, two
lines in `app/main.py` lifespan, four new tests.
</objective>
